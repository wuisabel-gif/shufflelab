ShuffleLab — single-shelf shuffle guessing game

Play against the mathematically optimal complete-feedback strategy of Clay, Kuba & Tripathi (arXiv:2607.10418). At every step the app shows the optimal next guess and P(correct | observations).

ShuffleLab is an independent reference implementation / research playground. The underlying mathematics (optimal strategy, exact distribution, mean, variance, CLT, large deviations, phase transitions) is due to Alexander Clay, Markus Kuba and Raghavendra Tripathi, On card guessing after an asymmetric single-shelf shuffle, arXiv:2607.10418 (2026). This page does not constitute an official companion implementation by the authors.
