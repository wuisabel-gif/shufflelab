ShuffleLab interactive

A single deck is shuffled once by an asymmetric single-shelf shuffle with parameter p. You then guess the cards one by one with complete feedback. Play against the mathematically optimal strategy and watch it explain every move.
The mathematics is due to Alexander Clay, Markus Kuba & Raghavendra Tripathi, On card guessing after an asymmetric single-shelf shuffle, arXiv:2607.10418 (2026), building on the position matrix of Kuba (arXiv:2602.12928) and Tripathi (arXiv:2602.07920). This page is an independent reference implementation — not an official companion by the authors.

How the shuffle is built

Cards 1…n−1 are each sent up (top) with probability p or down (bottom) with probability 1−p; card n is the peak. The deck (top → bottom) is [up-cards in increasing order], n, [down-cards in decreasing order] — always unimodal.
